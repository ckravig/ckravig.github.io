In 2024, I set out to design and install a residential solar photovoltaic system on my home as a self-directed engineering project. My goal was to gain practical experience in electrical systems, construction, and regulatory compliance—skills I wouldn’t normally encounter in my professional work.

I designed and built the system from the ground up, beginning with load calculations and system sizing, followed by researching national and local building and electrical codes. This included interpreting relevant sections of the National Electrical Code (NEC) for photovoltaic systems, as well as local permitting requirements.

After completing the design phase, I sourced all system components—including solar modules, inverters, racking, and electrical hardware—and planned the installation to ensure structural integrity, electrical safety, and code compliance.

The project required coordinating multiple disciplines: electrical design, construction planning, permitting, and hands-on installation. Through this process I developed a deeper understanding of photovoltaic system architecture, grid-tied power systems, and residential electrical infrastructure.

This project reflects my interest in learning complex systems through hands-on experimentation and research, and demonstrates my ability to independently design and execute a technically challenging project from concept to deployment.
